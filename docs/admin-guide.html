Administrator & Staff Handbook

Running your college on CampusOne

CampusOne is a modular college-management system: a core of academic structure and RBAC that every college gets, plus modules — Admissions, Fees, Hostel, Library, Placements and more — enabled per your institution's plan. This guide walks a new college through first-time setup, explains who can do what, and gives a module-by-module reference for day-to-day use.

Getting started

CampusOne's academic data has a strict shape: a Section only exists inside a Batch, a Batch only inside a Program, and so on. Set these up in order the first time — after that, most of your time is spent in Students, Attendance, Fees and the other day-to-day modules, not here.

flowchart LR
    A["Institution\nname & branding"] --> B["Academic Year\ne.g. 2025-26"]
    B --> C["Departments"]
    C --> D["Programs\ne.g. B.Tech CSE"]
    D --> E["Batches\ne.g. 2023-2027 intake"]
    E --> F["Sections\ne.g. CSE-A"]
    F --> G["Subjects, Faculty, Rooms"]
    G --> H["Timetable"]
    H --> I["Students"]
    I --> J["Day-to-day modules"]
  1. Set your institution's name and branding. Institution is a single record — your college's display name, URL slug, and an optional accent color used across the console.
  2. Create the current academic year and mark it current. Academic Years Every Batch is anchored to one, e.g. 2025-26, and only one year can be "current" at a time — creating a new one doesn't delete the old year's records.
  3. Add your departments. Departments These are the top-level academic units (Computer Science, Mechanical, Commerce…) that Programs and Faculty attach to.
  4. Add programs under each department. Programs A degree offering with a code and duration in years, e.g. B.Tech CSE, 4 years.
  5. Add batches. Batches One intake year-group of a program, tied to the academic year it started in — e.g. the 2023 CSE intake, batch name 2023-2027.
  6. Add sections within each batch. Sections The actual class a student and a timetable belong to, e.g. CSE-A, with its own current semester and seat capacity.
  7. Add subjects, faculty and rooms. Subjects · Faculty · Rooms Subjects are scoped to a program and semester; adding a Faculty record also creates that person's login.
  8. Build the timetable. Timetable Pairs a section, subject, faculty member and room to a weekday and time — the system rejects a slot that double-books a faculty member or room.
  9. Bring in your students. Students or Import / Export Add students one at a time, or paste a CSV to preview and commit a bulk import.
  10. Turn on the modules your plan includes. Core setup, Academics and RBAC are always on. Admissions, Fees, Hostel/Transport, Library and Placements are enabled per tenant by CampusOne — contact your account contact if a module you expect isn't in the sidebar yet.

Signing in & roles

Sign in with the email and password your Super Admin issued you. Every permission in CampusOne is checked as "does this role hold this permission inside your college" — a role never reaches across institutions. The table below is what each role can broadly reach; the exact grants are visible to Super Admin/College Admin under Roles & Permissions.

RoleTierWhat they doConsole access
SUPER_ADMIN Admin Full control of the college's CampusOne account: every module, billing, API keys, roles. Full
COLLEGE_ADMIN Admin Same reach as Super Admin — the day-to-day principal/registrar-level account. Full
DEPARTMENT_ADMIN Department Manages one department's structure, academics and college-operations records. No billing, API keys, roles or audit log. Broad
HOD Department Head of Department — same reach as Department Admin, scoped to running one department day-to-day. Broad
EXAM_ADMIN Department Owns the exam lifecycle — verifying and publishing marks — plus read access to Reports. Exams & Reports
FACULTY Staff Marks attendance, approves student leave, manages their own assignments and marks entry, posts announcements and class-group messages. Teaching tools
STAFF Staff Read access across academics, plus front-office work — documents, hostel/transport/library records, certificate issuance. Front office
STUDENT Self-service Requests leave, submits assignments, reads their own marks/attendance/announcements. No console yet
PARENT Self-service Reserved for guardian-facing visibility into a student's record. No console yet

A tenth role, PLATFORM_ADMIN, belongs to CampusOne's own operations team and spans every college — it never appears inside your account.

Common workflows

A handful of tasks end to end, the way they actually get done.

Admissions staff

Turn an applicant into an enrolled student

  1. Open Admissions, find the applicant, and advance their status one stage at a time as each check clears.
  2. Once they're Accepted, open the application and use Enroll — assign a roll number and (optionally) a section.
  3. The system creates their real record in Students automatically, linked back to this application.
Faculty

Run today's attendance for a class

  1. In Attendance, start a new session for your section and subject (today's date is the default).
  2. Mark each student present, absent, late or excused, then Submit the session.
  3. A department admin or HOD locks it once it's final. After that, fixing a mistake goes through a correction request instead of re-editing.
Exam Admin

Publish results for an exam

  1. Create the exam and add a schedule per subject in Examinations.
  2. Faculty enter marks against the schedule while it's in Draft, then submit.
  3. Verify the submitted marks, then Publish — SGPA and CGPA recompute for every affected student the moment marks go live.
Finance staff

Record a student's fee payment

  1. Raise an invoice against the student in Fees, using a fee structure if one matches their program.
  2. Record each payment as it comes in — the invoice's status (pending/partial/paid/overdue) recalculates automatically.
  3. Use an adjustment for a discount or fine, or a waiver to close out the balance without a payment.
Department Admin

Notify one department about a schedule change

  1. Open Announcements, choose Department as the audience and pick the department.
  2. Set a priority and, if it's time-sensitive, an expiry date so it stops showing after the fact.

FAQ & current limitations

Can we turn a module on ourselves?

Not yet — module enablement is set on your account by CampusOne. If a module in this guide isn't showing in your sidebar, contact your account contact to have it turned on for your plan.

Do payments actually move through Fees or Billing?

Both are internal ledgers today, not a live payment gateway. Recording a payment updates the invoice's status correctly, but no money actually moves through CampusOne yet — UPI support is planned.

Can students or parents log in?

The roles exist and their permissions are defined, but there's no student/parent-facing console yet — this guide covers the staff-facing admin console only.

Where do uploaded documents and certificates actually live?

A Document or Certificate record today stores a reference (a URL/description), not an uploaded file — there's no file storage behind it yet.

Is two-factor login or session management available?

The platform supports it, but there's no in-console settings screen for it yet. Contact your account contact if you need it enabled for a user.

Something looks wrong, or a module I need isn't listed here.

Reach out to your CampusOne account contact — this guide reflects what's live today and gets updated as new modules ship.
